How a day works

One day = 10 cards from the main course, then the supplements that use the same words: flashcards, a phrasal verb, an idiom, a sentence pattern, a joke — and writing once a week. They live on the same day page, not in a separate menu.

Course data comes from the real course files in the EverydayEnglish repo: A2 has 30 units, B1 has 26, B2 has 20. Foundation (60 days) and A1 (180 days) are complete; their lessons are listed from their course files.
